    Blocking hours

    Times follow your computer’s local time. An end earlier than the start continues into the next day. Equal times mean off.

    Lockdown mode

    During blocking hours, you can add sites or expand hours. Removing sites, shortening hours, and turning lockdown off must wait until unlock.

    Back up your settings

    Save your sites, hours, and lockdown setting in a file you can restore later.

    Replace your settings?

    This replaces your current sites and schedule. Lockdown rules still apply.
